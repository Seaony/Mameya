# 404 页面的插画：迷路的银喉长尾山雀

The attached image is the brand mascot of Mameya: a round, fluffy white shima-enaga (Japanese long-tailed tit). Draw the SAME character in the SAME style (smooth soft-gradient vector illustration with soft fluffy white feathers, tiny black bead eyes with one white highlight, small dark beak, soft pink cheeks, warm brown and grey wing, long dark grey tail), but in a new pose for a website 404 page.

- Pose: the little bird stands on its two tiny feet, holding a small round magnifying glass up in front of one eye with one wing, searching for something it cannot find. The magnifier has a soft warm wood-colored handle and a pale clear glass.
- Expression: a little lost and puzzled but still cute: curious wide eyes, eyebrows not needed, a tiny tilted head. Never sly, smug or sad.
- Outline: the whole character must form a compact vertical oval like the digit "0" (it sits between two big "4" digits): the long tail hangs straight down behind the body instead of sticking out sideways. Height about 1.4 times the width.
- Composition: portrait 2:3 canvas, the character centered and filling about 85% of the height, a little empty margin around it. No ground, no shadow, no other objects, no text, no letters, no numbers, no question marks.
- Background: one perfectly flat solid neutral grey (#9a9a9a), no gradient, no vignette, no texture, so it can be cut out cleanly.
